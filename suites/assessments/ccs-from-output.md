# CCS 4 km (UCLA-ROMS + MARBL), 2013: assessment from a direct scan of the restart files

Source: `phase5/ccs-scan/` only (summary.txt, levels.csv, extremes.csv, inventories.csv, bands.csv) plus `scan_restarts.py`. 103 restarts, 77.6 M ocean cells per file. Instantaneous snapshots, so tides alias into every series. "Inferred" marks anything the numbers do not show directly.

## 1. Summary

The physics looks healthy. Salt is conserved, there are no NaN/Inf in any variable in any file, deep T/S drift is negligible, and the vertical and lateral structure is smooth and sensible. The tidal signature (domain-mean zeta swings -0.4 to +0.6 m between snapshots, a 14-16 day kinetic-energy cycle, Cook Inlet at +/-4 m) suggests tides are in the run. The biogeochemistry is not clean. Between 2013-03-29 and 2013-04-02, at the march_ccs to april_ccs boundary, every major dissolved tracer (NO3, PO4, SiO3, O2, DIC, ALK) drops ~1% domain-wide while T, S, Fe and the refractory DOM pools do not move. This is the dominant problem: it is not physical and it contaminates every "annual drift" number. Second, the initial biology was far from equilibrium: small-phytoplankton, zooplankton and phosphorus pools relax by 60-93% over the year and the P:C ratios start wrong. Third, unconstrained tracer undershoot has grown steadily, so by December 18-36% of all ocean cells hold negative phytoplankton or zooplankton, and 32% hold negative DOC. Fourth, river-mouth cells (Fraser/Salish Sea especially) carry extreme, unphysical carbonate and nutrient values. pH is not computed on 2,251 columns, and ABIO pH is all zero.

## 3. Most important issues, ranked

1. **Tracer step at the Mar 29 to Apr 2 restart boundary** (march_ccs to april_ccs): ~1% in NO3/PO4/SiO3/O2/DIC/ALK, bottom-heavy (DIC -43 at depth), ≈-15 PgC, with T/S/Fe unchanged. Check how april_ccs was initialised.
2. **Initial plankton state and P:C stoichiometry** are out of equilibrium; sp/zoo/P pools fall 60-93% and diat/diaz C:P start at 11 and 4. Treat Jan-Mar biology as spin-up and fix the IC P pools.
3. **Spreading negative tracers** (36% diatC, 26% diazC, 22% spC, 18% zooC, 32% DOC, 2% NH4 by December) indicate no positivity control; small mostly, but local minima reach -19.7 mmol C/m3.
4. **Fraser/Salish Sea river forcing** produces ALK 9,100, DIC 8,800, DOC 990 mmol/m3: the carbonate system there is unusable until river BGC end-members are checked.
5. **pH diagnostics**: ABIO_PH_SURF all zero; 2,251 columns have pH = 0 in every file; ALT_CO2 fields are exact copies of the base tracers.
6. **Negative O2** in Borderland basins (-11.5) and growing offshore in the south (-6.2 by Dec), plus -154 in a shallow estuary cell. The OMZ undershoot and ventilation need attention; the <20 mmol/m3 volume (9% of domain) should be benchmarked.
7. **Localised artifacts**: PWS salinity spike (36.1) with SiO3/Fe negatives, diazP anomaly on the north boundary row, sub-freezing T in Cook Inlet (65 files), >3 m/s currents in SE Alaska channels.
8. **Slow ongoing drifts**: DOC/DON/DOP -7 to -8% and still falling, +1.1 degC interior SST year-end versus start, deep warming 0.0145 degC/yr. Small, but the run is not in equilibrium.

## 4. What this scan cannot tell

- Skill against observations: all realism statements (OMZ extent, SST change, nutrient and carbonate profiles, river end-members) rest on prior expectations, not data.
- Spatial patterns: only per-level means, extremes locations and four edge bands. Where negatives, the step or OMZ changes sit horizontally and vertically is unknown, as is whether the 2,251 pH columns are the grid rim.
- Time structure: only snapshots (daily to February, then weekly), so tidal aliasing, bloom timing and short events (the PWS spike) are not resolved. Task boundaries were checked only between scanned files.
- Fluxes and budgets: no air-sea CO2, river, or burial terms, so inventory changes cannot be attributed to physics, sources or the restart change.
- Causes: why april_ccs differs (IC, restart handling, BC file) and why P is mis-scaled need the run inputs.
- Volume and land-mask effects: land-mask pooling means band means include shallow cells at deep s-levels.
