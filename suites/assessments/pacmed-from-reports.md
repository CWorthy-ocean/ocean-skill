# PACMED 12 km "pyesper" run (2011-2013) - assessment from `mid-run-check.pdf` (MR, 15 pp) and `final-report.pdf` (FR, 30 pp)

## 1. Summary
The report shows 36 monthly restart snapshots (2011-01-31 to 2013-12-31) of a basin-scale 12 km Pacific run. It resumes from an earlier run's restart (PAC_v2-pyesper), so this is not a cold spin-up, and the boundary files were switched in Nov 2011. Large-scale physics and tracers are in good shape: against WOA23 the run-mean fields have correlations of 0.92-0.997 at the surface and about 0.96-0.99 at 200 m. Temperature surface bias is -0.17 C and salinity bias -0.01. Domain-mean tracers are nearly flat for three years, with only small drifts (a few tenths of a percent). The clear model-level problems are:
- Oxygen too high in the thermocline, with a thin oxygen minimum zone (OMZ): +16.9 mmol/m3 at 200 m, and the OMZ is about 57 m too thin.
- Weak subsurface seasonal variability for nutrients and oxygen (sigma ratio 0.5-0.6 at 200 m).
- Surface DIC about +23 to +26 mmol/m3 high.
- A poor fit to satellite chlorophyll (correlation 0.23-0.54) and to the satellite SSH variability (correlation 0.32, sigma ratio 4.3, probably tide aliasing).
- Surface alkalinity extremes at SE Asian river-mouth cells (43 and 5541 mmol/m3) that are not physical dilution results.
- Small negative tracers.

Evidence on the coast, rivers, boundaries and conservation is thin.

## 3. What the reports cannot tell me
- Rivers and coasts: only two river-mouth extremes are shown; no discharge, river concentrations, plume salinity, shelf biogeochemistry or nearshore oxygen anywhere in the domain. Nothing on Peru/Humboldt upwelling beyond one T-S box.
- Open boundaries (west near 95E, south near 55S, Atlantic/Caribbean part of the domain) and the Nov 2011 boundary-file switch: no discontinuity check, and the Atlantic portion looks absent from the comparison panels.
- Inventories and conservation: no heat, salt, carbon, nutrient or oxygen budgets, no air-sea CO2 flux, no net primary production (not in restart files).
- Numerical stability: no timestep, CFL, max velocity or kinetic energy; no count of negative-tracer cells.
- Currents and transport beyond two Cravatte bands: no EUC transport, Kuroshio, ITF, or eddy kinetic energy; SSH variability cannot be separated from tides.
- Sea ice or freezing-point behavior (surface temperature as low as -2.64 C at high latitudes).
- Interannual behavior: 2011-2013 (La Nina to neutral) is compared with a multi-decadal climatology, with no ENSO diagnostics.
- Deep ocean: nothing below 500 m except the OMZ column minima; no deep-water drift.
- Bio and carbon detail: no pCO2, aragonite saturation, DOC/POC, other phytoplankton or zooplankton pools, or iron observations.
- Temporal variability at fixed sites (time series), and any in-situ comparisons (Argo profiles, moorings, cruises).

## 4. Report quality notes
- FR p12 (Fig. 6) says "the report used GOSML instead; see the note on the first page" but page 1 is configuration only and has no such note.
- Raw config keys leak into user text (e.g. "defaults.period" on MR p4 and "defaults.section_depths" in FR Fig. 8's text).
- The "mid-run-check" and "final-report" cover the same 36 snapshots ending 2013-12-31, though the run is configured to 2015. "Mid-run" and "final" thus have no distinct time coverage.
- The MR domain-trends heading is alone on its own page (MR p4), with the figure on p5.
- Several colorbars saturate (differences, currents, nitrate +3, OMZ thickness -150), and the Taylor/target figures after Fig. 23 (FR p30) are dominated by the SSH outlier (target plot at about (4, 5)), compressing all other points near the origin.
- Surface and 100 m or 200 m points are indistinguishable on the Taylor/target plots (no depth key).
- MLD metric rows show "n/a" in the Notes column. In the T-S plots WOA temperature is in situ and the others potential (noted in the text, not corrected).
- Section difference panels saturate (oxygen beyond +30, nitrate beyond +/-3) and the section panels have tiny titles.
